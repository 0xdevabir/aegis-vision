A wanted face is
flagged in seconds.

  • YuNet detector finds faces even when they are small or turned at an angle
  • 128-d face code compared with every watchlist entry
  • Runs in separate worker processes, so the face work never crashes the video
  • A match sets off a red box, an alarm and an alert on every channel
  • Gender and age estimates are added to each capture

Rickshaw · 01 · Why we need it

Main roads choke. Nobody can prove who did it.

⚠️The problem today

🛣️

Battery rickshaws enter main roads, causing jams and accidents

👮

Traffic police cannot watch every road all day

🔁

Repeat offenders are hard to identify

📂

No record to prove a violation happened

→

✅With Aegis

🛰️

Every registered rickshaw carries a tracker powered by its own battery

✏️

Admins draw allowed zones and banned roads on a map

🚨

Leaving a zone sends a dashboard alert, an SMS to the owner and an e-fine

🧾

Every fine comes with a GPS trail as proof, plus the full history

Rickshaw · 02 · How it works

Five steps, fully automatic.

STEP 1

Tracker in the battery

A sealed box with GPS and a 4G SIM, powered by the rickshaw's own battery

STEP 2

Location every 10 s

It sends its position to the Aegis server over mobile data

STEP 3

Geo-fence check

Each new position is checked against the allowed zones and banned roads

STEP 4

Exit = alert + fine

A red alert appears on the map, the owner gets an SMS, and an e-fine is issued with GPS proof

STEP 5

Anti-tamper

It also raises an alert if the box is opened, unplugged or stops reporting
